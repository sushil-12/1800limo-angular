Vehicle Photo Guide
6 Required Angles
1
Front View
Stand directly in front, center the vehicle, keep full hood and bumper visible
2
Front Side Angle
45° from front-left — shows front grille and driver's side profile
3
Rear Side Angle
45° from rear-right — shows rear lights and passenger side
4
Rear View
Square up with the rear bumper, full trunk and taillights visible
5
Interior Front
From rear seat looking forward — dashboard, front console, and seats
6
Interior Rear
From front seat looking back — passenger cabin and seating space
Lighting
  • Exterior: Shoot between 8–10 AM or 2–4 PM with the sun behind you for even, shadow-free light.
  • Interior: Shoot on a cloudy day or in open shade — avoid harsh shadows from direct sunlight through windows.
  • Night shots: Turn on all interior lights, ambient lighting, and laser effects for dramatic effect.
  • Choose attractive backdrops — hotels, waterfront, scenic roads, or clean lots with clear sky.
Orientation & Framing
  • Always use landscape mode (phone held horizontally). Portrait images will be rejected.
  • Keep the full vehicle in frame — no cut-off bumpers, wheels, or rooflines.
  • Hold the camera at door-handle height for the most flattering exterior angle.
  • Minimum image size: 800 × 600 pixels. Higher is better.
Avoid These Mistakes
  • Blurry or out-of-focus images
  • Portrait (vertical) orientation for exterior shots
  • Partial vehicle — bumper, wheel, or roof cut off
  • Dark or underexposed photos
  • Dirty vehicle, garbage in background, or distracting clutter
  • Duplicate angles in multiple slots
