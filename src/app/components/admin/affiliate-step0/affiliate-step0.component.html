  • Follow the professional taxi, limo, and bus associations guidelines. Work the legal number of hours per day and use cell phones responsibly by law.

  • Follow all booking details and instructions to the letter. Failure to do so is a “thumbs down.”
  • You will be rated with a Thumbs Up or Down after each booking…its more logical. No ambiguous star ratings. Fix the issue after one thumbs down.
